Privacy Policy

Dama Ta! · MMC Solo Dev · Last updated 12 September 2026

The short version: everything you do against the computer — every difficulty level, the Analysis Board and the 3D board — runs on your device and is never uploaded. Online play is optional and needs an account you create in the app; what you enter there is stored on our game server and used only to run online games, ratings and tournaments. Ads in the free mobile version come from Google AdMob, and purchases are handled by your app store. We never sell your data.

Who this covers

This policy applies to the Dama Ta! Filipino checkers app published by MMC Solo Dev, on every platform it ships on:

  • Android — com.marjoncajocon.damata
  • Windows — MMCSoloDev.DamaTa
  • Web version

It also covers the Dama Ta! game server that the online features talk to, which we operate ourselves.

Playing offline: nothing leaves your device

Games against the AI, the Best Move hint, the Analysis Board, Game Review and the 3D board are computed entirely on your device by the built-in engine. No move, position, rating or result from offline play is sent to us, and the app contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

The app never asks for your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera. On Android it requests INTERNET and network-state permissions (for online play, ads and purchases) and a Wi-Fi multicast permission (only for finding opponents on your own local network — see Local-network play below).

Information stored on your device

This data is saved locally so the app works between sessions. It stays on your device, is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data, and is not transmitted to us unless a row below says otherwise.

Device identifier

On Android the app reads the system ANDROID_ID value (on iOS a Keychain identifier) and uses it as part of the encryption key for the local data above. This ties that data to the device it was created on, so a copied data folder simply fails to decrypt elsewhere. It is never stored in a readable form and is not used for advertising or tracking.

Online play: your account

Multiplayer, tournaments, online ratings and player profiles need an account, which you create inside the app. Offline play never requires one.

What we collect at sign-up

  • Required: a username and a password. The password is stored only as a salted hash; we cannot read it back.
  • Optional, and never shown to other players: your email address, full name, address, country, date of birth and gender. You can leave any of these empty. The email address is unverified today; it is kept so that a password-reset feature can use it later and so one address cannot register more than one account.
  • The guest ID of the device you signed up from, so one device makes one account.

What other players can see

Your public profile shows your display name, your online rating and the trophies you have won. Opponents see your display name and rating during a game, and anyone browsing the public lobby or a tournament sees it in the lists. Finished online games — the players' display names, the moves, the result and the rating change — are kept on the server and can be opened and replayed by other players from game lists, profiles and tournament standings. Nothing you entered as optional sign-up information is ever shown to anyone.

Gameplay and technical data

While you play online, your moves and board state go to our server to run the game and to resolve disconnections, draws and results. Like any networked service the server processes your device's IP address to deliver responses; it also counts failed sign-in attempts per address, in memory only, to slow down password guessing. Our server sits behind Cloudflare, which terminates the secure connection and processes connection metadata under Cloudflare's privacy policy. We do not build profiles from IP addresses and do not keep access logs beyond ordinary operational troubleshooting.

Where it is kept and for how long

Account data, online game records, tournament results and trophies are stored in our game server's database for as long as your account exists. Sign-in sessions expire on their own and are deleted when you sign out. Live match state and the reconnect association are held in memory only and discarded when the match ends.

Deleting your account

There is no in-app delete button yet. To delete your account, email us from the address below with your username. We remove the account, its credentials and every optional detail you gave us within 30 days. Records of finished online games are kept so that your former opponents' histories stay intact, but they are no longer linked to your personal details.

Local-network play

You can also play someone on the same Wi-Fi without an account. The app uses local-network discovery (multicast) to find the other device and then talks to it directly. Those games never pass through our server, and nothing about them is sent to us.

Advertising

The free mobile version shows ads supplied by Google AdMob: a banner on the game screen, and optional rewarded ads you can choose to watch to unlock a feature. Every rewarded ad is announced first and can be declined. The web version shows ads through Google AdSense.

To serve ads, Google may collect and process data including your advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, and device information. That processing is Google's, governed by their policies, not ours — we never see it. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

The Windows version shows no ads at all. It is sold as a paid app, and the advertising code is only active on mobile and web.

Your choices

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland: the app shows Google's consent form on first launch, before any ad is requested. You can change or withdraw that choice at any time using the Privacy options entry on the home screen.
  • Anywhere: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings — on Android, under Settings › Privacy › Ads.
  • Remove ads permanently: the optional one-time Premium purchase disables all advertising.

Ads are limited to a PG content rating, because this is a general-audience board game.

Purchases and app integrity

Dama Ta! offers one optional, one-time Premium purchase that removes ads and unlocks unlimited feature credits. The transaction is handled entirely by Google Play or the Microsoft Store; your payment details go to them and never reach the app or the developer.

To confirm a Google Play purchase is genuine, the app sends the purchase token to our server, which checks it with Google Play; the token is used only for that check. On Android the app may also request a Play Integrity token from Google Play services and have our server verify it, to confirm the app is an unmodified copy on a genuine device. Neither token is stored.

Children

Dama Ta! is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the optional date of birth at sign-up is not used to target anything. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. Data leaves our hands only as described on this page — to Google (ads, purchase verification, Play Integrity), to Cloudflare (as the network in front of our server), to the app store that handles your purchase, and to other players in the limited profile and game form described above — or where the law requires it.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what account data we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Everything stored only on your device is yours to clear from the History screen or by uninstalling the app.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated and the "last updated" date at the top will change with it. Material changes will also be noted in the app's store listing release notes.
