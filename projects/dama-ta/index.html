Filipino draughts

Dama Ta!

Filipino dama with the strongest net in the family — a 512-wide NNUE.

NNUE neural network

Get it on Google PlaySee the screens

The engine

The evaluation is an NNUE neural network — 512 hidden units over 128 input features, 4 phase buckets on piece count — written in C and compiled straight into the app. It is trained from self-play by a Go trainer, quantised to 16-bit integers, and evaluated incrementally so the search can afford to call it at every node.

The rules of this variant

Filipino dama on an 8×8 board. Men move diagonally forward; kings fly any distance along a diagonal. Capturing is mandatory and you must take the line that captures the most pieces.

What you get

  • Play offline against a neural-network engine — no account, no connection
  • A real 3D board rendered with three.js, alongside the classic 2D board
  • Analysis board with an evaluation bar and best-move hints
  • Game review: step back through a finished game move by move
  • Custom position setup, pass & play on one device, and AI vs AI
  • Board and piece themes, and a rules panel for the variant
