Puzzleshire: forty-seven games in one cosy app.

Puzzle, card and board games in one place: Sudoku and logic puzzles, solitaires, arcade classics, word games, and Filipino favourites like Tong-its, Pusoy Dos, Sungka and Dama against the computer. Everything plays offline, with no ads and no accounts.

What you get

  • 47 games in one app, each with several difficulty levels or board sizes.
  • A daily challenge in every game: the same puzzle for everyone that day, with a streak for each day in a row you win one.
  • Puzzles that are always fair. Levels are generated by the game itself and checked by a solver before you see them: logic puzzles have exactly one answer, and solitaire deals and box puzzles are proven winnable.
  • Hints and undo wherever they make sense, and your game is saved when you leave, so you can continue later.
  • Computer opponents at Easy, Medium and Hard for the board and card games, or play a friend on the same PC.
  • Colour-blind friendly: pieces and tiles carry a symbol as well as a colour.
  • Offline, private, ad-free. No accounts, no tracking; your progress stays on your PC.

How it is made

Every game is written in C++ on SDL2, one file per game, and every picture and sound is drawn and synthesised in code. There are no image or sound files, and no art or levels taken from other games. A small Flutter app hosts the games on Windows and handles the Microsoft Store.

Privacy. Puzzleshire collects nothing. Read the full privacy policy.
